Errors in the original schematic
================================

Sheet 1, U13 and U14 have all the pin names and numbers mixed up. Sometimes
D and R are swaped, and the order is wrong.

Sheet 7, U95 is shown as pin 9 input and pin 6 output, but it's actually
pin 8 that is the output of the buffer at pin 9.

Sheet 7, PIA has the pin numbers for CB1 and CB2 swapped. Shown as CB1 (19),
and CB2(18), but in reality it's CB1(18) and CB2(19).

R9 on the PCB only has its wiper connected, and pin 3 is not connected. In the
schematic it is. As it's functionally identical, and all the other pots have
both pins connected, the version presented here has this fixed.

Sheet 8, U67 has pins 12 and 13 misnumbered. The PCB has 13 on top, and 14
at the bottom.

The last page of the schematics PCB shows the component placement. R43 and
R44 are swapped, and the order of R35-R41 is all wrong.
